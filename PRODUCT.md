# ElSewedy GenZ Coders

ElSewedy GenZ Coders is a project-first youth technology academy for Egyptian students aged 10 to 18. The platform helps students enroll in live courses, attend embedded Zoom sessions, submit work, earn XP and badges, and share projects with parents, schools, and friends.

Register: brand and product.

The experience should feel energetic, trustworthy, and concrete: students see what they will build, parents see structure and progress, schools see a serious partner, and admins can operate the academy at scale.
